## It Started Small

It started with projects.

A client had a problem. I built a solution. Then came the next project.

But over time, I started asking myself:

**What if I could build something that keeps creating value after the project is finished?**

That changed how I think about building.

Projects have an ending.

Products can keep evolving.

That's why today, I'm more interested in building **systems, products, and businesses** than simply completing projects.

I'm not just trying to finish the work.

**I'm trying to build something that lasts.**
